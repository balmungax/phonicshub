📖 The Rule: A or An?

Both A and An are indefinite articles — they mean "any one" or "one of many". The choice depends on the SOUND of the next word, not the letter!
Оба слова (a и an) — неопределённые артикли. Они означают "какой-то, один из многих". Выбор зависит от ЗВУКА следующего слова!

A

Before consonant sounds
(before согласных звуков)

a cat · a dog · a ball
a house · a university

An

Before vowel sounds: a e i o u
(before гласных звуков)

an apple · an egg
an igloo · an hour ← silent h!
🎵
Sound = first sound you HEAR, not the first letter!
"university" starts with u but sounds like /juː/ → use a university
"hour" starts with h but sounds like /aʊ/ → use an hour
💡
When to use A / An:
• First time you mention something: "I see a cat."
• Jobs / professions: "She is an artist."
• One of a group: "He is a student."

📖 Three More Rules

Now we learn when to use the (pointing to something specific) and when to use nothing at all (the zero article)!
Теперь учим когда использовать the (указываем на что-то конкретное) и когда артикль вообще не нужен!

A / An

First mention — any one thing
Первое упоминание

I saw a cat. (any cat)

The

Known thing — both know which
Конкретная вещь

The cat is sleeping. (that cat!)

— Zero

General — all things in general
Вещи вообще

— Cats are cute. / I like — milk.
🎯
When to use THE:
• Already mentioned: "I bought a bike. The bike is red." (we know which bike!)
• Unique things: the sun, the moon, the Earth
• Both people know it: "Close the door, please." (you can see the door!)
🚫
When to use ZERO article (nothing!):
• General plurals: — Dogs are friendly. (all dogs in the world!)
• Uncountable nouns in general: I like — milk / — music
• Country & city names: — Russia, — Moscow (but: the USA, the UK!)
• Meals, sports, languages: — breakfast, — football, — English

📖 Tricky Cases & Common Mistakes

Some words look like they should use a but actually need an — and vice versa! The sound is what matters, not the letter. Also: Russian speakers often make specific mistakes with articles!
⚠️
Tricky A words (look like they need "an" but don't!):
a university (/juː/ — starts with "y" sound) • a European (/j/) • a one-eyed cat (/w/) • a unicorn (/j/)
⚠️
Tricky AN words (look like they need "a" but don't!):
an hour (/aʊ/ — h is silent!) • an honest boy (h silent) • an FBI agent (/ef/ — F sounds like "ef") • an x-ray (/eks/)

⚠️ Most Common Mistakes for Russian Speakers

❌ Mistake 1: Forgetting "the"

Wrong: I saw movie yesterday.

Right: I saw the movie yesterday. (or a movie)

В русском нет артиклей, поэтому мы часто забываем их!

❌ Mistake 2: Using "the" for all things in general

Wrong: The dogs are friendly animals. (meaning all dogs)

Right: — Dogs are friendly animals.

Когда говорим о всех животных в целом — без артикля!

❌ Mistake 3: "The" with country names

Wrong: I live in the Russia.

Right: I live in — Russia. (but: the USA, the UK, the Netherlands)
