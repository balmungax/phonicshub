🎭 The Comic Strip Slide 1
"And he who does NOT understand, raise your hand. — Manolito, what is it you don't understand? — From September until now, NOTHING!"
Manolito has been confused since September — the whole semester. Look at the faces of the other students!
📖 Check Understanding Slide 2
1. What does the teacher ask students to do if they don't understand?
The teacher says: "And he who does NOT understand, raise your hand." He invites students to openly signal their confusion rather than staying silent.
2. What does Manolito mean by "From September until now, nothing!"?
He means he hasn't understood anything since the school year began in September. He feels completely lost and overwhelmed in class.
3. How do you think Manolito feels in this moment?
He likely feels embarrassed, frustrated, and scared — but also a little brave. It takes courage to admit in front of everyone that you understand nothing.
4. Why might the teacher be surprised or concerned?
Because Manolito hasn't understood anything for months — since September. The teacher realizes there is a serious learning gap that was never caught or addressed.
Pair discussion: If YOU were the teacher, what would you do next to help Manolito?
