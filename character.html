🎭 The Comic Strip – Manolito

Original comic: “And he who does NOT understand, raise your hand. — Let's see, Manolito, what is it that you don't understand? — From September until now, NOTHING!”

📸 Image dimensions: 2170 × 725 (wide panel). Manolito’s confession: a whole semester without understanding.

📖 Check understanding

1. What does the teacher ask the students to do if they don't understand?
The teacher says: “And he who does NOT understand, raise your hand.” He wants students to openly show their confusion.
2. What does Manolito mean by “From September until now, nothing!”?
Manolito means he hasn’t understood ANYTHING in class since the school year started in September. He feels completely lost.
3. How do you think Manolito feels in this moment?
Manolito likely feels embarrassed, frustrated, scared, and maybe a bit brave for being honest. He feels overwhelmed and confused.
4. Why might the teacher be surprised or concerned?
Because Manolito hasn't understood anything for months — since September. The teacher realizes there is a serious learning gap.

Pair discussion: If you were the teacher, what would you do next to help Manolito?

✍️ Creative Story Mission

🎯 Based on the comic strip, write a short story (150–300 words).

Your story must include:

  • 🔹 A main character (Manolito or a similar student).
  • 🔹 A clear setting (where & when the story takes place).
  • 🔹 The character's feelings (describe how they feel at the start, middle, and end).
  • 🔹 Use at least 5 words from the vocabulary table (English or Russian).

📌 Answer these key questions in your story:

Does the teacher help him? If yes, how? (extra lessons, kind words, different method?)

Does another student help? (a classmate, a tutor, a friend?)

Does the main character find a way to understand? What changes?

Write the resolution. How does the problem end? Does Manolito finally understand something?

💡 Example starter: “Manolito felt frustrated and embarrassed after raising his hand. But the teacher, Mrs. Garcia, smiled and said, ‘It’s brave to admit confusion. Let’s find a solution together…’”

Possible teacher help: one-on-one tutoring, visual aids, patience.
Possible peer help: study buddy, explaining in simple words, group work.
Resolution idea: Manolito masters a topic, feels relieved, and raises his hand again – but now to answer!

Extension: Share your story with a partner. Did the main character change from confused to confident? Highlight the vocabulary you used.
